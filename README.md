## 🌌 What Is Resonance Vault?

Resonance Vault 2026 is a Windows-native desktop environment for transforming, organizing, and archiving audio collections. It reads a wide spectrum of input formats, re-encodes them with careful attention to fidelity, embeds structured metadata, and files everything into a vault-like library structure that makes rediscovery a pleasure rather than a chore.

Think of it as a master gardener for your music: it prunes formats that no longer serve you, grafts clean metadata onto stems that had grown wild, and plants your tracks in neat rows so future-you can find them instantly.

The suite is designed around three quiet promises:

1. **Fidelity first.** Nothing is thrown away that shouldn't be.
2. **Clarity always.** You see what's happening at every stage.
3. **Ownership forever.** What lives in your vault is truly yours.

---

## 📜 The Origin Story

The project began as a weekend experiment in 2024 — a small utility that a handful of audio enthusiasts used to settle an ongoing debate: *does format really change the way a song feels?* The answer, it turned out, was "sometimes, yes, and that's fascinating." That curiosity snowballed into a modular toolkit, then into a full desktop environment, and eventually into the vault you see today.

By early 2026, the suite had matured into a stable, warmly received workspace used by hobbyists, archivists, and casual listeners alike. This repository carries that lineage forward with documentation, guides, and release history.

## 🗝️ Why a Vault, Not a Converter?

Conversion implies a one-way door. A vault implies belonging. The distinction matters because the best audio tools don't just produce files — they create a *place* for those files to live and grow.

When you use Resonance Vault, you're not merely pushing data through a pipeline. You're stocking a cellar. Each batch you process is labeled, dated, catalogued, and cross-referenced so that six months from now, when a half-remembered melody drifts into your head, you can walk to the correct shelf and pull the exact bottle you were craving.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

Below is the map of capabilities that make Resonance Vault what it is. Each item is a constellation point — small on its own, but together they form a recognizable sky.

### 🎼 Core Audio Engine
- Broad-format ingestion covering dominant lossy and lossless containers
- Sample-rate and bit-depth preservation where the source permits
- Batch processing with intelligent queue management
- Metadata extraction, normalization, and re-embedding (title, artist, album, year, track number, artwork)
- Gapless-aware encoding for albums that demand continuity
- Volume levelling using perceptually weighted analysis
- Waveform preview for quick sanity checks

### 🗂️ Vault Organization
- Automatic folder taxonomy based on artist / album / year patterns you define
- Duplicate detection using acoustic fingerprinting, not just filename matching
- Smart collections that update themselves as new material arrives
- Tag-based query language for power users
- Exportable catalogue snapshots in plain, human-readable formats

### 🎨 User Experience
- Responsive layout that reflows gracefully from small laptop screens to ultrawide monitors
- Light, dark, and "amber archive" themes
- Keyboard-first navigation for those who dislike reaching for the mouse
- Drag-and-drop staging area with instant conflict resolution prompts
- Progress ribbons that show both per-file and per-batch status

### 🧠 Intelligence Layer
- Suggested filename corrections based on audio fingerprint lookups
- Anomaly alerts when a track's metadata contradicts its acoustic profile
- Recurring task scheduler for people who like their archiving to happen quietly overnight
- Context-aware upgrade prompts that never interrupt a running job

### 🔐 Trust Features
- Fully offline processing by default — nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly enable it
- Signed release artifacts for every stable build
- Transparent change logs published with each version
- No telemetry, no accounts, no shadow profiles

## 🛠️ Setup Walkthrough

Setup is intentionally gentle. You don't need to be a systems administrator to get going.

1. **Launch the installer.** A welcome screen greets you and offers language selection.
2. **Choose your vault location.** Point it at a drive with room to grow. External SSDs work beautifully.
3. **Pick a theme.** Light, dark, or amber archive. You can change this later.
4. **Optional: connect your library.** Point the tool at any folder of audio files to begin cataloguing.
5. **Review privacy settings.** Everything is offline-first; toggle anything you'd like to change.
6. **Finish.** The vault initializes, and you're dropped into the main workspace with a short guided tour.

First-run usually takes under two minutes.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an internet connection to use the suite?**
No. Everything works offline. Connectivity is only used if you explicitly enable optional features.

**Will it overwrite my original files?**
Only if you tell it to. By default, originals are preserved untouched.

**Can I run it on multiple machines?**
Yes — the license is per-user, not per-machine.

**Does it collect any data about me?**
No telemetry, no silent reporting, no usage tracking of any kind.

**What happens if I lose my vault configuration?**
A built-in recovery tool can rebuild the catalogue from the files themselves.

**Is there a portable version?**
A portable mode is on the roadmap for late 2026.
